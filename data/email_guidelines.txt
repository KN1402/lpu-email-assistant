University Professional Email Guidelines

1. Email Subject
The subject should clearly describe the purpose of the email.
A short and specific subject is preferred.

2. Greeting
A professional email should begin with an appropriate greeting.
Examples include:
Dear Professor,
Dear Sir/Madam,
Dear Dr. [Name],

3. Introduction
The first paragraph should clearly explain why the student is writing the email.

4. Main Message
The main part of the email should contain the important details.
The message should be clear, concise, respectful, and relevant to the purpose of the email.

5. Requests
When requesting something from a professor or university staff member, the request should be stated politely.
The student should briefly explain the reason for the request when appropriate.

6. Assignment Extension
An assignment extension request should mention:
- The assignment or course
- The amount of additional time requested
- A brief reason for the request
- A polite request for consideration

7. Leave Request
A leave request should mention:
- The reason for leave
- The required dates or duration
- A polite request for approval

8. Closing
A professional email should end with an appropriate closing.
Examples include:
Yours sincerely,
Regards,
Thank you,

9. Email Tone
University emails should generally use a professional, respectful, and polite tone.
Avoid slang, unnecessary abbreviations, offensive language, and overly casual expressions.

10. Email Length
Emails should be concise and contain only information relevant to the purpose of the communication.
